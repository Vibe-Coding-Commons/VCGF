# Exception management

A control may not be ignored silently. Every exception records: Control ID, reason, risk, risk owner, compensating control, approval, created date, expiration date, review date, and status. Expired exceptions are invalid. A compensating control must address the same risk outcome rather than merely document acceptance.

Use `templates/security/security-exception.md` as the project record format.
