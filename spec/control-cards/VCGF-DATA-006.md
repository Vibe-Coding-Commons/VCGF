# VCGF-DATA-006 — Data in Transit Protection
MUST · data-protection · profiles: high-assurance, production · full text: controls/data-protection/vcgf-data-006-data-in-transit-protection.md

## Requirement
Sensitive data and authentication material MUST use authenticated encrypted transport across untrusted networks.

## Guidance
Disable insecure transport paths, validate peer identity, and avoid moving secrets or personal data through URLs or other leaky channels.
