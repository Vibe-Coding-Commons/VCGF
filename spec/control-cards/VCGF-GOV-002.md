# VCGF-GOV-002 — Human Approval Gates
MUST · governance · profiles: high-assurance, production · full text: controls/governance/vcgf-gov-002-human-approval-gates.md

## Requirement
High-risk and critical changes MUST receive explicit human approval before execution when they affect authentication, authorization, destructive data operations, secrets, production infrastructure, payments, personal-data processing, or security controls.

## Guidance
Use a documented risk classification. The AI agent must stop before the irreversible or security-sensitive action, describe the proposed change, risks, rollback, and required evidence, and record the approval reference.
