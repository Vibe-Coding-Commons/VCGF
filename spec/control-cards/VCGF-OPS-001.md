# VCGF-OPS-001 — Environment Separation
MUST · operations · profiles: high-assurance, production · full text: controls/operations/vcgf-ops-001-environment-separation.md

## Requirement
Development, test, staging, and production environments MUST separate sensitive credentials, data access, and deployment authority according to risk.

## Guidance
Do not use production secrets or personal data in lower environments unless explicitly authorized and protected with equivalent controls.
