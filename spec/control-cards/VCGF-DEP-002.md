# VCGF-DEP-002 — Locked and Reproducible Dependencies
SHOULD · dependencies · profiles: high-assurance · full text: controls/dependencies/vcgf-dep-002-locked-and-reproducible-dependencies.md

## Requirement
Projects SHOULD use supported lockfiles or equivalent mechanisms to make dependency resolution reviewable and reproducible.

## Guidance
Do not delete or regenerate lockfiles casually during unrelated work. Major upgrades should be isolated from unrelated changes.
