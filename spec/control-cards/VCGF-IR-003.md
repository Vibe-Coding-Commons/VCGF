# VCGF-IR-003 — Personal Data Incident Handling
MUST · incident-response · profiles: high-assurance, production · full text: controls/incident-response/vcgf-ir-003-personal-data-incident-handling.md

## Requirement
Projects processing personal data MUST define containment, scope assessment, evidence preservation, decision ownership, and applicable notification or legal-review paths for suspected personal-data incidents.

## Guidance
Do not encode jurisdiction-specific notification deadlines into the generic core; regulatory overlays may add them.
