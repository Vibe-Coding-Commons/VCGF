# VCGF-AUDIT-003 — Audit Tamper Resistance
SHOULD · logging-auditing · profiles: high-assurance · full text: controls/logging-auditing/vcgf-audit-003-audit-tamper-resistance.md

## Requirement
High-assurance systems SHOULD protect critical audit records from unauthorized modification or deletion.

## Guidance
Use access separation, append-oriented storage, retention controls, monitoring, or integrity mechanisms appropriate to the threat model.
