# VCGF-SECR-002 — Secret Rotation and Compromise Response
MUST · secrets · profiles: high-assurance, production · full text: controls/secrets/vcgf-secr-002-secret-rotation-and-compromise-response.md

## Requirement
Secrets and cryptographic credentials MUST have documented rotation and emergency revocation procedures.

## Guidance
A suspected exposed secret must be treated as compromised: rotate or revoke it, assess logs and dependent systems, and invalidate derived credentials where appropriate.
