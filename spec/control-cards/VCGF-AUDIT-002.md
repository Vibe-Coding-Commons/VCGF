# VCGF-AUDIT-002 — Privileged and Business Audit Trail
MUST · logging-auditing · profiles: high-assurance, production · full text: controls/logging-auditing/vcgf-audit-002-privileged-and-business-audit-trail.md

## Requirement
Security-sensitive administrative, approval, permission, recovery, data-export, and critical business actions MUST produce attributable audit evidence where the project risk requires it.

## Guidance
Record actor, action, resource, timestamp, result, and correlation context while excluding secrets and unnecessary personal data.
