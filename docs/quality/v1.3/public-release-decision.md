# VCGF v1.3.0 — Owner public framework release decision

**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Decision:** The owner authorizes publication of the VCGF **framework source distribution** as v1.3.0 after repository-native validation passes on the exact release candidate commit.

## Scope

The v1.3.0 release integrates **VCGF Smart UI Components & Interaction Standards v1.0.0**, including selective Components, Interaction Patterns, Screen Compositions and Journey Patterns, together with their governance, schemas, tests, adapters, traceability and linked Calendar/Searchable-Select standards.

## Non-waiver

This decision does **not** relabel unexecuted target-application acceptance scenarios as PASS, does not certify production deployments, and does not waive trusted-host, provider, accessibility, usability, dependency, recovery or other applicable evidence requirements.

**Production admission remains BLOCKED** until the applicable cumulative evidence gates are satisfied.
