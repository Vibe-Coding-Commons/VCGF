# Limitations

- Knowledge and instruction files shape agent behavior; they are not an application-runtime enforcement boundary.
- Platform security scans are evidence inputs, not proof of complete VCGF conformance.
- VCGF does not treat Lovable instructions or scans as a substitute for application-runtime authorization, validation, encryption, or operational controls.

Unsupported or external controls must not be silently represented as native enforcement.
